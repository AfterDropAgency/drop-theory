Sample audit

What a teardown looks like.

Every application starts with a look at your store, your ads and your follow-up. Here is the kind of thing we find, from the public pages of a real apparel store (name withheld).

  • The shipping promise does not matchThe product page says 2 to 3 day shipping. The shipping policy says 7 to 14 business days. Cold ad traffic notices that.
  • No safety net on a first orderAll sales are final, with no exchanges, on a product sold in four sizes. That is a lot to ask of a first-time buyer.
  • Trust details left unfinishedA policy page still shows a placeholder date, and care instructions were copied from a different product.
  • Nothing to prove the productTwo photos and no reviews or customer content. The heavyweight fabric is the hook, but nothing shows it.
  • Pieces sold one at a timeA matching top and bottom are separate listings with no set option. A bundle lifts order value, which decides how much ad spend a store can afford.

What we would test first: the weight and feel of the fabric on video, the fit on real people, and a matching-set drop. Based on public pages only. Ads, pixel and cart follow-up get checked with you on the call.
